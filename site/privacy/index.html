Habbits — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-04

Habbits is a local-first habit tracker. Your data lives on your device. The developer runs no server and operates no backend: there is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your habits and completions are stored locally on your device in an SQLite database. Aside from features you control (see below) and your device's own operating-system backup (Android Auto Backup or iOS device backup, which copy app data to your own cloud account), this data stays on your device.

Optional file export and import

You can export your data to a JSON file via the system share sheet, and import it again later. Where that file goes is entirely your choice; Habbits does not upload it anywhere.

Data sharing

The developer does not collect, sell, or share any of your data with third parties.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its on-device data (a copy may remain in your device's own operating-system backup until that backup is cleared).

Children

Habbits is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children.
